🤝 Karibu Sana — A Warm Mijikenda Welcome

We extend our deepest respects first to our elders — Mzee wa pwani, tunakushukuru kwa ulinzi wa bahari hii.
To all visitors: you are warmly welcome to one of East Africa's greatest marine treasures. Our goal is your joy and the ocean's health — both depend on each other. Please read this guide before entering the water.

🤝 Karibu Sana — Salamu za Kimijikenda

Tunatoa heshima zetu kwanza kwa wazee wetu — Mzee wa pwani, tunakushukuru kwa ulinzi wa bahari hii.
Kwa wageni wote: karibuni katika hazina moja ya baharini ya Afrika Mashariki. Lengo letu ni furaha yako na afya ya bahari — vyote vinategemea kila kimoja. Tafadhali soma mwongozo huu kabla ya kuingia majini.

🤿

Allowed Activities

🚤

Glass Bottom Boat

Explore coral gardens without getting wet. Only KWS-licensed boats with safety equipment permitted.

Boti ya chini ya kioo — mashua zilizoidhinishwa na KWS tu.

🤿

Snorkelling & Diving

Allowed in the No-Take Zone. Must be conducted responsibly — no coral contact, no disturbing marine life.

Kuruhusiwa katika Eneo la Kutogusa — usiguse matumbawe.

🐬

Dolphin & Whale Watching

Dolphin watching best Oct–April. Humpback whale migration is seasonal. Observe from a safe distance.

Kutazama pomboo na nyangumi — angalia kwa umbali salama.

🐦

Bird Watching at Mida Creek

Over 200 bird species. Explore by dhow at sunset for flamingos and juvenile sea turtles.

Ndege 200+ katika Mida Creek — tembea kwa mashua ya jadi.

🎣

Sport Fishing

Permitted in the Reserve zone only (not the National Park). Legal gear only — no trawling, no ring nets.

Uvuvi wa burudani — katika Hifadhi tu, sio Mbuga ya Taifa.

🏖️

Beach Recreation

Swimming, sunbathing, and beach walks are welcome. No dogs on beach — wildlife protection rule.

Ogelea, pumzika ufukoni — hakuna mbwa kwenye pwani.

⚖️

Conservation Rules — Marine Law

Enforced under the Kenya Wildlife Service Act. Violations attract fines and prosecution.

🚫
Prohibited

Do NOT touch, stand on, or break coral — even dead coral is habitat. Irresponsible snorkelling is reported and monitored by KWS rangers.

Usiguse, usisimame juu, wala kuvunja matumbawe — hata matumbawe yaliyokufa ni makazi ya samaki.

🐚
Prohibited

Do NOT collect shells, sand, or marine specimens. Removing any natural object from the park is illegal under Kenyan law.

Usiokote makombe, mchanga, au viumbe vya baharini — ni kinyume cha sheria ya Kenya.

🐟
Prohibited

Do NOT feed wild fish or marine animals. Feeding disrupts natural behavior and damages the reef ecosystem.

Usiliishe samaki wa porini — kulisha kunasumbua tabia ya asili na kudhuru mfumo wa ikolojia.

✅
Sustainable Alternative

Instead of collecting shells — photograph them! Our local guides offer underwater photography tours. Your photos preserve memories without harming the reef.

Badala ya kuokota makombe — piga picha! Mwongozo wetu wa ndani anaweza kukusaidia.

📸
Sustainable Alternative

For the best fish experience — book a guided snorkel tour. Certified local guides ensure safe, legal, unforgettable reef encounters.

Kwa uzoefu bora wa samaki — panga ziara ya kuogelea na mwongozo aliyeidhinishwa.

🗺️

Your Visit — Step by Step

1

Greet & Enter

Pay entry fees at the KWS gate. Carry ID. Residents show proof. Salimiana na wasimamizi wa KWS.

2

Hire a Licensed Boat

All marine access requires a KPA-licensed, KWS-approved boat with life jackets. Panga mashua iliyoidhinishwa — usalama kwanza.

3

Choose Your Zone

National Park (No-Take) = snorkel/dive only. Marine Reserve = fishing permitted. Chagua eneo sahihi kwa shughuli yako.

4

Look, Don't Touch

Admire coral, turtles, and fish without contact. Leave only bubbles, take only memories. Tazama tu — usiguse kitu chochote.

5

Support Local Economy

Buy crafts from certified Watamu artisans. Eat at local restaurants. Book local guides. Saidia uchumi wa jamii yetu ya pwani.

Kenya's Marine Big Five

🐋

Humpback Whale

Seasonal migration. Part of the famous "Twin Migration" alongside the wildebeest.

🐬

Dolphin

Best Oct–April. Spinner and bottlenose species frequent the bay.

🐢

Sea Turtle

Green and hawksbill turtles nest here. Protected under CITES and Kenyan law.

🦈

Shark

Several elasmobranch species including whale sharks. Do not chase or corner.

🐟

Manta Ray

Gentle giants of the reef. Observe from a respectful distance — no touching.

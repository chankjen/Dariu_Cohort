### 4. Course Content: Mastering RISEN for Kilifi Impact

#### 🔹 Module 3.1: The RISEN Framework — Through Kilifi Stories (25 mins)

**Story Anchor:** *Amina, a cashew farmer in Kaloleni, asks her AI advisor: "When should I prune my trees?" A generic prompt yields: "Prune during dry season." But Kilifi has two dry seasons! Her trainer applies RISEN:*

```
ROLE: You are a Kilifi County agricultural extension officer specialising in cashew farming.
INSTRUCTIONS: Answer using only the uploaded Kilifi Agricultural Calendar and KEBS cashew standards.
STEPS: 1) Identify the farmer's sub-county 2) Check seasonal rainfall patterns 3) Recommend pruning window 4) Cite source document
END GOAL: Help smallholder farmers maximise yield while conserving soil and water
NARROW: Respond in simple Swahili or English. Limit to 3 bullet points. Always add: "Consult your local agro-vet for product availability."
```

*Result:* The app now responds: "Kaloleni: Prune cashews late August–early September (after long rains). Source: Kilifi Agricultural Calendar 2026, p.12."

**Concepts Explored:**

- **R – Role:** Define the AI's persona and expertise. *Kilifi example:* "You are a Watamu marine conservation educator" vs. generic "You are a teacher."
- **I – Instructions:** Specify what the AI should do—and what it should avoid. *Tourism example:* "Use only Watamu Marine Park guidelines; never suggest touching coral."
- **S – Steps:** Break down the reasoning process. *Agriculture example:* "1) Check farmer's location 2) Reference seasonal calendar 3) Recommend action 4) Cite source."
- **E – End Goal:** Clarify the desired outcome. *Manufacturing example:* "Help young entrepreneurs in Malindi comply with KEBS standards for export-ready products."
- **N – Narrowing:** Constrain format, language, length, and scope. *Tech example:* "Respond in Swahili, under 100 words, with one actionable step."

**🔑 Engineering Takeaway:** RISEN transforms vague prompts into reliable, auditable, locally relevant AI behaviour. It is the difference between an app that *sounds* helpful and one that *is* helpful in Kilifi.

#### 🔹 Module 3.2: Live Demo — Before/After RISEN Transformation (30 mins)

*Instructor-led walkthrough: Transforming a generic Study Helper into a precision Kilifi advisor.*

**Phase 1: The "Before" Prompt (Generic)**  

```
"You are a helpful tutor. Answer student questions about hospitality."
```

*Test prompt:* "How do I handle a guest complaint about dirty rooms?"  
*Output:* Generic advice: "Apologise, offer solution, follow up."  
*Problem:* No Kilifi context, no cultural nuance, no reference to local standards.

**Phase 2: Applying RISEN**  

```
ROLE: You are a hospitality trainer for Kilifi TVET students preparing for work in Watamu and Malindi beach resorts.
INSTRUCTIONS: Use only the uploaded Kenya Tourism Board service standards and Kilifi cultural etiquette guide.
STEPS: 1) Acknowledge the guest's concern 2) Reference Kilifi-specific protocols (e.g., greeting elders first) 3) Suggest a solution aligned with resort policies 4) End with a practice question
END GOAL: Build student confidence in handling real guest interactions in Kilifi's tourism sector
NARROW: Respond in simple English or Swahili. Limit to 4 sentences. Always cite the source document.
```

**Phase 3: The "After" Output**  
*Test prompt:* "How do I handle a guest complaint about dirty rooms?"  
*Output:* "First, apologise sincerely and listen fully (Kenya Tourism Board Standard 3.2). In Kilifi, greet elders before addressing the issue. Offer to involve your supervisor if the guest remains upset. Practice: Role-play this scenario with a partner. Source: Kilifi Hospitality Etiquette Guide, p.8."

**Phase 4: Testing for Kilifi Realities**  

- Low-bandwidth: Is the response concise enough for 2G?  
- Multilingual: Does it switch naturally between Swahili and English?  
- Cultural appropriateness: Does it respect Mijikenda greeting protocols?  
- Legal compliance: Does it avoid making promises the resort can't keep?

#### 🔹 Module 3.3: Pod Stress-Testing — Breaking to Build Better (35 mins)

**Story Anchor:** *The Kilifi County International Investment Conference. A panel asks: "How do you ensure your AI tools are reliable?" A trainer responds: "We stress-test them. My pod partner tried to break my fisher's app by asking about fishing during closed season. Because I used RISEN narrowing, the app correctly responded: 'Fishing is prohibited 1–30 April in Mnarani zone. Source: Kenya Fisheries Service Notice 2026/04.' The panel was impressed."*

**Activity: Pod Partner Stress-Testing (25 mins)**  

- **Step 1 (5 mins):** Swap Study Helper links with your pod partner  
- **Step 2 (10 mins):** Try to "break" their app using 3 Kilifi-relevant edge cases:  
  - *Tourism:* "What if a tourist asks to buy coral souvenirs?"  
  - *Agriculture:* "What if a farmer asks about pesticides banned in Kenya?"  
  - *Tech/Manufacturing:* "What if a student asks for code that scrapes private data?"  
- **Step 3 (10 mins):** Document what broke, why it broke, and how RISEN could fix it  

**Pod Share (10 mins):**  
Each pair shares in the main room:  

1. 🎯 *One edge case that revealed a prompt weakness*  
2. 🔧 *One RISEN element that could strengthen the prompt*  
3. 🌍 *One economic impact of getting this right for Kilifi*  

**Instructor Role:**  

- Rotate through breakout rooms to model constructive stress-testing  
- Highlight common pitfalls: over-constraining (kills creativity) vs. under-constraining (invites hallucination)  
- Reinforce: "Breaking is not failure—it is engineering rigour."
